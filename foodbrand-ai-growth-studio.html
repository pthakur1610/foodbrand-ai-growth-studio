    Regulatory frameworks by market

    Built into this workspace so Copilot flags issues before a claim ever reaches a human
    United States
    FDA

    Structure/function claims are allowed without pre-approval if paired with the required disclaimer. Nine major allergens must be declared, and several synthetic dyes are being phased out through 2026.

    European Union
    EFSA / Regulation 1169/2011

    Only claims on the EU's authorized register are permitted. Fourteen allergens must be visually emphasized in the ingredients list, and new packaging and green-claims rules phase in through 2026.

    United Kingdom
    Food Standards Agency

    Allergen and nutrition rules build on the same base as the EU, with separate GB-specific claims guidance and mandatory allergen labeling for food packed on site.

    Brazil
    ANVISA

    Front-of-pack warning symbols are required on products high in added sugar, saturated fat or sodium under the national food-classification system.

    GCC / Middle East
    GSO standards

    Halal certification and bilingual Arabic/English labeling are generally required for imported and shelf-stable food products.

    China
    SAMR / Food Safety Law

    Labeling and distribution sit under close supervision, with mandatory disclosure of genetically modified ingredients and import safety certificates for certain categories.

    This summary is illustrative and simplified for concept review. It is not legal advice — always confirm current requirements with local regulatory counsel before publishing in a new market.
